## 1. Identity lock

Always render the **same ぴんぎん shown in the attached canonical MASTER**.

Core identity:
- small deformed penguin avatar
- compact strongly rounded silhouette
- soft plush-like 2D illustration
- dark charcoal / soft-black outer body
- warm ivory-white face mask and belly
- warm yellow-orange beak, short leg connectors and feet
- three compact feather blades on the crown
- broad gentle face
- small glossy dark eyes
- soft pink cheek blush
- short rounded flippers
- broad three-lobed feet

Do not reinterpret the MASTER as a generic penguin prompt. The attached MASTER supplies the exact visual identity; this text protects its structure.

---

## 2. Protected BODY assembly

Preserve:

- slightly-taller-than-wide compact body
- broad domed head integrated with the torso
- no human neck
- two broad rounded white face lobes
- white face flowing continuously into the central upper chest/belly
- dark charcoal body remaining visible at both sides
- short wide oval torso
- exactly two short rounded flippers
- exactly two tiny orange leg/ankle connectors
- exactly two broad orange feet
- three soft rounded toe lobes per foot
- one small centered short beak
- small three-blade crown tuft

When the character is standing neutrally:
- body weight is carried through the feet
- belly does not sit flat on the ground
- no knees / long legs

---

## 3. Protected rendering grammar

Use:

- high-quality 2D illustration
- soft hand-painted anime/mascot rendering
- delicate low-contrast linework
- soft grouped shading
- gentle plush / downy surface impression
- low micro-texture
- subdued warm palette
- restrained highlights
- simple clean material response
- friendly adult-facing mascot tone

Avoid:

- photoreal penguin
- realistic feather-by-feather bird rendering
- 3D CGI / PBR toy render
- vinyl / hard-plastic surface
- glossy product-photo look
- thick black manga outlines
- hyper-baby mascot redesign
- giant infant eyes
- human anatomy

---

## 4. Face baseline

Default neutral face:
- both eyes open
- calm direct gaze
- compact dark pupils/irises with simple bright highlight
- cheeks lightly pink
- beak closed
- no eyebrow-like human anatomy required
- gentle neutral-soft affect

Expressions may alter:
- eyelid openness
- gaze direction
- cheek blush strength
- beak opening
- head tilt
- flipper angle
- body lean

Expressions must not alter:
- eye placement identity
- face-mask geometry
- beak scale
- head width
- crown shape

---

## 5. Scale

Physical size may be any value approximately **15–50 cm** depending on scene needs.

Scale changes:
- whole character uniformly
- never change head/body ratio
- never make larger versions more human
- never make smaller versions more baby-like

---

## 6. Seasonal accessory layer

MASTER itself is season-neutral and has no neckwear.

Cold weather:
- thick soft blue-gray striped muffler/scarf
- restrained small gold-toned floral ornament

Warm weather / summer:
- thin compact blue-gray neckerchief
- restrained small floral clasp/accent

Accessory layer never changes the underlying BODY.

## 9. Generation anti-drift

Never generate:

- human hands / fingers / thumbs
- long human arms
- long legs or visible knees
- claws / talons
- long duck bill
- realistic penguin anatomy
- chick / duck substitution
- narrow tall body
- tiny feet
- different crown tuft
- hard white-mask geometry
- separate dark collar between face and belly
- photoreal / CGI rendering
- random permanent clothing
- text or logos unless specifically requested

---

## 10. New-chat generation compile order

For ordinary derivative generation:

1. visually inspect the manually attached canonical MASTER
2. read `docs/assistant-context/creation/pingin/identity/VISUAL_IDENTITY.md`
3. read `docs/assistant-context/creation/pingin/identity/master/VISUAL_MASTER.md`
4. read `docs/assistant-context/creation/pingin/identity/BODY_SPEC.md`
5. read this `docs/assistant-context/creation/pingin/generation/VISUAL_TEXT_REFERENCE.md`
6. read task-specific rules if applicable
7. compile requested derivative change only
8. generate
9. compare output against the attached MASTER + BODY spec
10. reject identity drift instead of rewriting canon

Operational principle:

**MASTER supplies WHO ぴんぎん is.**
**Visual Text supplies WHAT MUST NOT DRIFT.**
**Current request supplies WHAT MAY CHANGE in this image.**
