## 1. Protected physical scale

- full physical-size range: approximately **15–50 cm tall**
- there is no single mandatory default height
- every size inside this range is the same ぴんぎん
- scale change is uniform only; it never alters proportions

Within a continuous manga scene/sequence, relative size remains stable unless the scene explicitly changes it.

---

## 2. Whole-body silhouette

Protected shape:

- compact, strongly rounded penguin mascot body
- visually soft and plush-like, but still an active avatar character
- overall silhouette is slightly taller than wide
- broad rounded crown
- wide cheek/head region
- short rounded torso
- no human neck
- no human shoulder / chest / waist / pelvis articulation
- the body narrows only gently from the head into the lower torso
- center of gravity remains low and stable
- body is not resting directly on the ground in neutral standing; weight is carried through the feet

The head and torso are visually integrated into one compact character mass, while a soft lower-head / upper-torso transition remains visible.

Forbidden:
- tall/slim body
- narrow cylinder body
- separate giant sphere head + tiny torso
- human torso proportions
- obvious waist or hips
- long neck

---

## 3. Crown / head tuft

- crown is a smooth rounded dome
- a small top tuft is mandatory
- approved MASTER shows **three compact leaf-like feather blades**
- the three blades form a small fan, with mild natural asymmetry
- tuft remains secondary to the crown and never becomes a large crest

Forbidden:
- mohawk
- long plume
- many-feather crest
- sharp spiky anime hair

---

## 4. Face-mask to belly topology

The approved MASTER resolves the ambiguity that was hidden by the winter scarf in the earlier design sheet.

Protected topology:

- warm ivory-white face mask forms two broad rounded cheek lobes
- both lobes meet naturally around and below the beak
- the white lower face continues into the white upper chest / belly through a **soft continuous central transition**
- there is no dark collar band between face and chest
- a very subtle contour/shadow may separate chin from upper chest, but it is not a color boundary
- dark charcoal side plumage continues around both sides of the white front field

Forbidden:
- heart-shaped white patch
- circular face disc
- hard geometric mask edge
- permanent dark collar line
- decorative neck marking

---

## 5. Torso / belly

- torso is short, wide and softly oval
- belly is broad, centered and ivory-white
- belly remains visually full without becoming spherical or swollen
- charcoal body remains visible at both lateral sides
- lower torso clears the ground because the character stands on its feet
- no human abdominal contour
- no chest musculature
- no waist indentation

---

## 6. Flippers

- exactly two flippers
- attach high on the lateral upper torso
- each flipper is broad at the root, gently tapering toward a rounded end
- neutral MASTER posture: flippers hang downward with slight outward separation
- flippers are substantial and soft, not thin blades
- inner lower edge may show a narrow ivory rim where the front belly meets the flipper/body

Forbidden:
- hands
- fingers
- thumbs
- elbows
- human arms
- long wings
- separated flight feathers

---

## 7. Standing legs / feet

The approved MASTER establishes **foot-supported upright standing** as the neutral baseline.

Protected lower-body construction:

- exactly two very short orange leg/ankle connectors are visible beneath the lower body
- connectors are compact and mostly hidden by body mass
- no knees are visible
- no long shins
- feet are broad, rounded and planted on the ground
- feet point slightly outward from center
- each foot has **three soft rounded toe lobes**
- toe lobes are simplified; there are no claws, talons, scales or realistic webbing
- left/right feet are symmetric in design, with natural perspective variation allowed
- a clear central gap remains between the two feet

The feet carry the body's weight. The belly itself should not appear to be the primary ground-contact point in the neutral standing MASTER pose.

Forbidden:
- sitting-flat feet placement as the neutral MASTER baseline
- tiny feet
- realistic penguin claws
- human shoes/feet
- long legs
- visible knees
- bird talons

---

## 8. Beak

- one small centered warm yellow-orange beak
- compact rounded wedge / flattened oval-diamond form
- slightly wider than tall
- short projection
- upper/lower division may be lightly visible
- beak remains subordinate to the eyes / face

Forbidden:
- long duck bill
- hooked beak
- pointed raptor beak
- oversized chick beak
- realistic nostril-heavy anatomy

---

## 9. Rear / tail continuity

Rear-view continuity remains supported by the approved design-sheet reference:

- rear body is broad and rounded
- one small centered tail feature may be visible near the lower back
- tail remains compact and secondary
- side view may show only a small rear protrusion
- tail never becomes long, pointed or fan-shaped

The front MASTER does not override this rear-view continuity rule.

---

## 10. Canonical neutral standing posture

The protected neutral standing baseline is:

- exactly one ぴんぎん
- front-facing
- upright on both feet
- full body visible
- centered
- near-orthographic / minimal perspective distortion
- crown tuft fully visible
- flippers relaxed at the sides
- both short leg connectors and both feet readable
- neutral gaze / neutral-soft expression
- no lean
- no prop
- no text
- no logo
- no scene
- no neckwear
- no floral accessory
- white / warm-white background
- only a faint contact shadow allowed

This is the baseline for identity comparison, not a mandatory pose for derivatives.

## 11. BODY anti-drift gate

BODY FAIL if a derivative materially changes:

- overall rounded compact silhouette
- head/body integration
- crown tuft construction
- continuous face-mask / chest topology
- flipper geometry
- foot-supported standing anatomy when the neutral standing pose is requested
- three-lobed rounded feet design
- short-leg logic
- beak size / form
- scale-invariant proportions

Do not modify this BODY spec to match a failed generation.
