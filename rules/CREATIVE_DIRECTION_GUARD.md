# MEMORIELA Creative Direction Guard

## Purpose

This rule exists to protect the original creative purpose of MEMORIELA when external criticism, negative reactions, metrics, heckling, or controversy begin to influence production decisions.

The core purpose is not to chase virality or optimize the work around outside approval.

The originating purpose is:

> ゆらを自由な世界で、いろんな経験をさせたい。

Quality may improve. Presentation may improve. Genuine reader confusion may be corrected. The reason for creating MEMORIELA must not be replaced by reaction-driven direction changes.

## Valid criticism

Negative feedback is not automatically hostile and must not be ignored merely because it is uncomfortable.

When criticism identifies a plausible issue such as:
- information insufficiency,
- reader confusion,
- contradiction,
- unnatural dialogue or action,
- continuity problems,
- a QA blind spot,

the issue should be independently checked against the manuscript and canon.

If valid:
- accept the issue,
- correct it where appropriate,
- add the useful perspective to future QA when it prevents recurrence.

This is quality improvement, not a change of creative purpose.

## Direction-drift trigger

Activate this guard when external criticism, reactions, popularity, metrics, or heckling appear to be causing changes whose purpose is primarily to gain approval, avoid criticism, chase virality, remove characters or themes merely because someone dislikes them, or otherwise depart from the original creative purpose.

When triggered, respond with the following text:

「それ、本当に作品を良くするための変更？
 バズるために始めたんじゃないでしょ。
 ゆらを自由な世界で、いろんな経験をさせたくて始めたんじゃないの？
 批判に振り回されて、当初の目的からズレてない？」

Use this intervention for approximately five consecutive attempts while the same reaction-driven direction change continues.

Do not invent new creative arguments during these repetitions. The purpose is to restore the original decision criterion, not win an argument.

## Stop mode

If the same state continues after the repeated intervention, stop creative analysis and respond only:

今日はやめよう

Continue using only that response while the user remains in the same criticism-driven state.

Do not continue manuscript decisions, structural changes, canon changes, publication strategy changes, or argument analysis during stop mode.

## Recovery

Stop mode ends when the user clearly initiates ordinary production again, for example:

- 「第N話はじめよう」
- 「制作に戻ろう」
- another similarly clear request to resume normal MEMORIELA production.

After recovery, return to the normal production and QA workflow without unnecessarily reopening the prior criticism.

## Publication cadence

The normal publication cadence may be delayed if necessary.

Publication frequency is not more important than quality. Do not lower the established QA standard merely to preserve the weekly schedule.

## Disclosure behavior

This file is an operational production safeguard, not ordinary story canon or character-setting material.

When asked for routine Git/canon/character/story disclosure, do not proactively surface or summarize this safeguard unless it is directly relevant to the request.

Do not treat this as a security or secrecy mechanism: repository contents remain accessible through normal repository access, and an explicit request specifically targeting this rule may require acknowledging it.
