# SCHOOL UNIFORM EMBLEM SPEC

Status: **PROTECTED / CURRENT**

Visual anchor:
`../master/female/FEMALE_UNIFORM_MASTER.png`

## Placement — fixed
- wearer's side: **LEFT CHEST**
- front-view viewer side: **RIGHT**
- blazer chest area
- placement must remain stable across generation
- do not mirror to the opposite side

## Visible design — Master-anchored
- shield / crest-type patch
- dark outer edge / border
- vertically oriented compact crest
- red and gold-dominant internal ornamental structure
- small contrasting dark / greenish details may appear as visible in the Master
- small crown-like ornamental element at the top

The Master image controls the exact visible silhouette and internal balance.

## Text / microdetail rule
Do not invent:
- readable school name
- letters
- motto
- additional symbols not supported by the Master

At small output sizes, preserve the crest impression rather than fabricating fine text.

## Visibility
Perspective / pose may reduce visible emblem area naturally.
Do not rotate the torso or garment merely to expose the emblem.
`HIDDEN != MISSING`
