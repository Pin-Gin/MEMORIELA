# FEMALE SCHOOL UNIFORM VISUAL TEXT

Status: **PROTECTED / CURRENT / EXECUTION AUTHORITY**

Source Master:
`visuals/school-uniform/master/female/FEMALE_UNIFORM_MASTER.png`

Purpose:
承認済み女子制服Masterの見た目を、生成時に固定適用するためのVisual Text。

This is not a suggestion layer.
Do not reinterpret the uniform into a more common school-uniform design.

## 1. Overall silhouette
- traditional fitted blazer-based Japanese school-uniform direction
- clean, formal, restrained silhouette
- blazer follows a feminine fitted cut without changing the wearer's BODY
- waist shaping is present as garment tailoring only
- no oversized / boxy blazer reinterpretation
- no decorative fashion redesign

## 2. Blazer
- base color: muted medium-to-dark brown
- overall impression: slightly dark brown, not navy and not charcoal
- structured tailored blazer
- notched lapel
- black piping clearly follows the lapel / collar edge
- black piping continues along visible front-edge and pocket-flap accents
- two visible front buttons
- buttons: restrained warm gold
- two lower flap pockets
- clean sleeve construction
- white shirt cuffs may appear slightly from the sleeves
- no frill, lace, ribbon trim, decorative chain or ornamental border

## 3. Shirt
- plain white school shirt
- pointed collar
- clean, simple construction
- no visible decorative lace / embroidery
- collar remains readable beneath the ribbon and blazer

## 4. Ribbon
- grade-2 ribbon color: red
- large but school-uniform-appropriate bow
- central knot
- two lower ribbon tails
- deep / restrained red rather than vivid neon red
- ribbon remains centered at the shirt collar

## 5. Skirt
- pleated school skirt
- dark black-to-near-black base
- check pattern dominated by dark muted red lines
- restrained secondary dark lines may exist only as part of the Master appearance
- regular pleat rhythm
- no frill / lace / asymmetrical hem
- no green, beige or bright multicolor tartan reinterpretation
- canonical school length is governed by `SCHOOL_UNIFORM_SPEC.md`; mannequin proportions do not redefine character BODY or real garment-length measurement

## 6. Emblem
- located on wearer's LEFT chest
- appears on viewer's RIGHT in a front-facing image
- shield / crest-type patch
- dark outer border
- visible red and gold-dominant internal decorative structure with small contrasting details
- small crown-like ornamental element at the top
- treat the Master image as the visual anchor for the emblem silhouette and color balance
- do not invent readable school-name lettering or additional text
- do not mirror to the opposite chest

## 7. Legwear / shoes
Base full-uniform Master appearance:
- black socks
- black loafers
- loafers have a clean classic school-shoe silhouette
- glossy reflections remain restrained by the project-wide rendering style

Character-specific profile may override allowed legwear variants without redesigning the base uniform.

## 8. Material / surface
- blazer and skirt read as ordinary woven school-uniform fabric
- matte to low-sheen
- no satin / vinyl / leather blazer reinterpretation
- ribbon may retain mild fabric sheen but not glossy plastic appearance
- shoes may show natural leather-like highlight while remaining compatible with Matte Natural Anime

## 9. Prohibited reinterpretation
Do not:
- turn the blazer navy / charcoal
- change black piping to light piping
- change red ribbon to blue
- brighten gold buttons into vivid yellow metal
- simplify the skirt into a plain solid-color skirt
- change the red-black check into a green tartan
- remove / mirror / relocate the emblem
- redesign the blazer because another school-uniform convention is more common
- transfer mannequin BODY proportions to the character

## 10. Authority boundary
This Visual Text controls outfit appearance only.

Character-specific:
- BODY
- FACE
- EYE
- HAIR
- SKIN
- pose
- identity

remain controlled by that character's Visual Authority.

`GARMENT FOLLOWS CHARACTER BODY.`
