# MEMORIELA SCHOOL UNIFORM SPEC

Status: **PROTECTED / CURRENT TEXT SPEC**

高校制服はブレザー型。

## Female — fixed
- visual Master: `master/female/FEMALE_UNIFORM_MASTER.png`
- blazer: slightly dark muted brown
- piping: black
- buttons: subdued gold
- shirt: plain white school shirt
- grade-2 accent color: red
- ribbon: red
- skirt: black-base check with dark muted red lines
- skirt construction: standard pleated school skirt
- standard skirt length: approximately 7 cm above knee as the school baseline
- emblem: wearer's left chest / viewer-right in front view
- base full-uniform legwear: black socks
- base shoes: black loafers

The approved Female Uniform Master is the primary visual anchor for silhouette, visible color balance, piping, ribbon, skirt pattern and emblem appearance.

Mannequin proportions do not redefine garment-length measurement on a character.

## Male — fixed direction
- blazer: same brown family as female uniform
- tie: red
- slacks: black to dark charcoal
- socks: black
- shoes: black leather shoes

Male visual Master remains pending.

## Non-negotiable behavior
Do not replace the brown blazer with common navy / charcoal school-uniform conventions.
Do not replace red grade color with blue.
Do not add unauthorized frills, decorative trims, extra accent colors or redesign the check pattern.
Do not move or mirror the emblem.
Do not transfer mannequin BODY to a character.
