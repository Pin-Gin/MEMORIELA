## 2. Eyes

Status: **CURRENT PROTECTED BASELINE / FURTHER CONTROLLED VALIDATION MAY REFINE BY EXPLICIT REVISION**

### 2.1 Eye color — selected direction

- Base color: **blue-gray / 青灰色**
- Direction: gray remains present, but the blue component is **slightly stronger** than a neutral blue-gray.
- Saturation: restrained; do not become vivid cobalt, cyan, or jewel-blue.
- Brightness: medium-high to high enough to preserve a clear, translucent impression.
- Iris should contain subtle **pale silver-white / whitish reflection** that makes the eyes feel luminous and clear.
- The white emphasis refers mainly to the iris reflection / highlight / pale tonal variation, **not** to artificially enlarging the visible sclera.
- Overall impression: cool, clear, soft, calm, slightly luminous.

### 2.2 Eye size — selected direction

- Eye size should remain balanced with an adult woman's face.
- **Slightly larger than average**, but not large enough to become a childlike or oversized anime-eye design.
- The eye should read as expressive at first glance while still preserving mature facial proportions.
- Horizontal width should contribute more to the perceived size than excessive vertical opening.
- Preserve enough sclera to keep the eye readable, but do not use enlarged sclera as the main method for making the eye appear bigger.

### 2.3 Eye shape — selected direction

**SELECTED: A — mildly almond-shaped, slightly horizontally elongated, neutral to very gently downturned outer corners**

- Overall form: **ややアーモンド型**。
- Proportion: roundness is restrained; the eye reads slightly more horizontal than vertical.
- Outer corner: approximately level, or only **very slightly lowered** to soften the expression.
- Do not create a strong drooping-eye look.
- Do not create a sharp upward tilt or fox-eye look.
- Iris presence may be moderately strong, but eye opening itself must remain adult-balanced.
- The shape should communicate softness, calmness, refinement, and approachability rather than childish cuteness or sharp glamour.

### 2.4 Iris size — selected direction

- Iris size is **slightly larger than average** relative to the visible eye opening.
- The iris should have clear visual presence, but must not dominate the entire eye opening.
- Preserve visible sclera on both sides so the eye still reads as an adult woman's eye rather than a childlike oversized anime eye.
- Do not enlarge the iris so far that the sclera nearly disappears.
- Do not reduce the iris enough to create a cold, sharp, or hyper-realistic impression.
- Target impression: calm, clear, expressive, soft, and slightly luminous.

### 2.5 Eye aspect ratio / eyelid lines — selected direction

- Reference eye opening ratio: **horizontal width : vertical opening = approximately 2.6–2.8 : 1**.
- Treat this as a design target, not a rigid pixel measurement; perspective, expression, and eyelid motion may create small visual deviations.
- The eye should still read as slightly horizontally elongated rather than round.
- Upper eyelid line: **moderately defined**, slightly stronger than the lower eyelid, but not thick, heavy, or sharply graphic.
- Upper line should taper naturally toward the inner and outer corners.
- Lower eyelid line: **soft, light, and restrained**; avoid a fully enclosed dark outline around the eye.
- The lower lid should support softness and adult refinement without making the eye look sleepy or childlike.
- Avoid excessive vertical opening that would break the 2.6–2.8 : 1 target and create a round oversized-anime-eye look.

### 2.6 Inter-eye spacing — provisional geometric target

- Primary direction: **balanced; neither wide-set nor close-set**.
- Reference gap between the eyes: approximately **0.9–1.05 of one visible eye width** in a neutral, front-facing view.
- Treat this as a practical target range rather than a rigid geometric law.
- The visual priority is natural facial balance; do not let numeric adherence create an obviously wide-set or close-set appearance.
- Further controlled validation may refine this range only through an explicit approved FACE revision; until then this target remains the current generation authority.

### 2.7 Iris outer ring — selected direction

- The iris outer edge is **slightly darker blue-gray than the central iris**.
- The darker edge exists to keep the iris readable and defined without reducing transparency.
- Do **not** render it as a harsh black ring, thick outline, or colored-contact-lens effect.
- The transition from outer ring to inner iris should remain soft and natural.
- Priority: clear iris boundary + preserved softness + preserved translucency.

### 2.8 Pupil — selected direction with YURA-specific signature detail

- Pupil color: **very dark blue-gray to deep gray, close to black but not flat pure black**.
- Preserve a subtle cool blue-gray character even at the darkest value.
- Pupil size: natural / moderate; neither pin-small nor oversized.
- Shape: almost circular, but **very subtly vertically elongated**.
- YURA-specific identifying design: add an **extremely small teardrop-like notch on the lower-right edge of the pupil**.
- The notch should function as a discreet signature detail, not a decorative symbol.
- At ordinary viewing size, the pupil should still look natural. The identifying notch should become noticeable mainly in close-up views.
- Do not turn the pupil into a heart, star, obvious logo, or conspicuous fantasy symbol.
- This feature is intended as a visual identity marker / provenance-supporting design cue, not as a technical anti-copy mechanism.

The separately protected authority for this identifying detail is `docs/assistant-context/creation/yura/identity/eyes/EYE_SIGNATURE_SPEC.md`.

### 2.9 Eye highlights — selected direction A

- Use **one primary highlight + one very small secondary highlight**.
- Primary highlight position: upper area, preferably **slightly upper-right** in the neutral front-facing reference.
- Secondary highlight: much smaller, placed on the opposite side or slightly lower to support natural depth.
- Highlight color: **silver-white / pale white**, not a hard flat pure-white sticker-like mark.
- Highlights should reinforce translucency and clarity without making the eye overly sparkly, glossy, or childlike.
- Do not fill the iris with numerous starry reflections.
- Exact highlight position may shift naturally with lighting direction in scene generation, but the reference FACE MASTER should preserve the one-primary-plus-one-secondary structure.

### 2.10 Eyelashes — selected direction

- Upper eyelashes: **naturally somewhat fuller than average**, but not dense enough to resemble heavy mascara or false lashes.
- The upper lashes should follow the existing upper-eyelid direction rather than creating a separate graphic fan shape.
- Outer-corner lashes: **slightly longer than the central / inner lashes**, creating a subtle outward emphasis.
- The outer-corner emphasis must remain soft; do not create dramatic winged, fox-eye, or glamour makeup styling.
- Lower eyelashes: **very restrained and sparse**. They may appear as a few delicate indications rather than a continuous dense row.
- Inner-corner lashes should remain short and inconspicuous.
- Overall target: feminine and refined, but natural enough that the lashes do not become the dominant facial feature.

### 2.11 Anime stylization strength — selected direction

- Overall rendering is **anime-oriented**, but the eyes must not become an exaggerated symbolic anime-eye design.
- Preserve the adult-balanced proportions already defined above; do not enlarge the eyes, iris, or vertical opening merely to increase "anime" readability.
- Iris color gradients, silver-white reflections, highlights, and the YURA-specific pupil notch may be rendered with careful detail.
- Eyelid outlines and eyelashes should remain **comparatively simple and clean**, avoiding excessive graphic decoration.
- Avoid hyper-realistic wet-eye rendering, photographic capillary detail, or realism that conflicts with the established YURA illustration style.
- Avoid the opposite extreme of flat chibi / mascot-like eyes with simplified giant irises and minimal adult facial anatomy.
- Target balance: **refined anime illustration with controlled iris detail, restrained linework, adult facial balance, and soft natural readability**.
