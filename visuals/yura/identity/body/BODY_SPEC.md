## 1. Core identity / scale

- Character: 久遠ゆら / YURA
- Adult woman, apparent age: early twenties
- Height: **153 cm**
- Overall scale: **petite / 小柄**
- Build: **slender / 華奢**, but not skeletal
- Total body ratio: **7.25 heads tall as the protected design target**
- Adult proportions remain clearly readable; do not enlarge the head to communicate small stature.
- YURA should read as a compact adult woman, not a childlike or chibi proportion.
- Leg-length impression: **standard to slightly long**, not fashion-model elongated.

Magic phrase remains semantically authoritative:

`153cmの小柄・華奢 体格比で胸はやや豊かめ 露出は控えめ`

Interpretation of `体格比で胸はやや豊かめ`:
- relative to YURA's petite/slender frame
- not independently oversized anatomy
- preserve the slender ribcage

### 1A. Scale invariance / canvas independence — PROTECTED

**The 7.25-head ratio is independent of image size, canvas size and aspect ratio.**

When YURA is resized inside an image:

- total crown-to-sole figure height remains **7.25 × head height**
- scale the entire anatomy uniformly with one scale factor
- head, neck, shoulders, torso, bust, waist, pelvis, thighs, knees, calves, ankles, arms, hands and feet all scale together
- changing the canvas from landscape to portrait, portrait to landscape, square to portrait, or changing output resolution must not change anatomy
- changing how much of the canvas YURA occupies must not change anatomy
- changing print / monitor dimensions must not change anatomy
- do not make thighs / calves thicker merely because a taller canvas is used
- do not enlarge or shrink the head relative to the body because the figure is made smaller or larger on screen
- do not lengthen / shorten legs to fill vertical space
- do not stretch / compress the torso to fit the frame
- preserve all width relationships as well as vertical relationships

**Aspect-ratio adaptation rule:** absorb the difference with background / negative space / framing. If there is not enough room, make the whole character uniformly smaller rather than changing the BODY ratio.

A canvas-size request is therefore a **layout request, not a BODY-generation request**.

## 2. Neck / shoulders

- Neck thickness: **slender to standard** for an adult woman.
- Neck length: **standard to slightly long-looking**, enough for a refined / delicate impression without a fashion-model swan neck.
- Shoulder width: **somewhat narrow for an adult woman**.
- Shoulder line: natural adult-female gentle outward/downward slope.
- Do not make the shoulders strongly drooping / exaggeratedly sloped.
- Do not make the shoulders square, flat or athletic.
- Clavicles may be lightly readable, but never sharply bony.
- Neck → shoulder → upper-arm transition remains smooth and soft.

## 3. Ribcage / bust — APPROVED FULLNESS

### Ribcage

- Ribcage / thorax remains **slender and compact**.
- Do not widen the whole upper torso merely to support the bust.
- Front chest width remains balanced under the narrow shoulder line.
- Side thorax depth remains natural / modest.

### Bust volume

- Bust is **clearly to moderately fuller relative to the petite/slender frame**.
- This is the revised and approved fullness after the first BODY validation was judged too conservative.
- Preserve a clearly visible relative fullness while keeping YURA's entire body petite / slender.
- Do not change posture, camera angle, clothing tightness or cleavage to fake volume.

### Bust shape

- Baseline shape: **soft hemispherical / 半球型寄り**.
- Not a rigid perfect sphere.
- Upper contour: gently sloped / restrained fullness.
- Lower contour: naturally fuller and rounder.
- The approved direction has **slightly more lower fullness and natural forward projection** than the first controlled BODY candidate.
- Avoid implant-like upper-pole inflation, sharply conical form or gravity-defying placement.

### Placement / projection

- Vertical placement: natural adult-female position.
- Front width: may read slightly fuller but must not make the whole upper torso broad.
- Prefer natural depth / lower fullness over excessive lateral spread.
- Side projection: **moderately to clearly pronounced for YURA's petite frame**, while remaining proportionate.
- Base transition into the ribcage must remain smooth rather than pasted-on.

## 4. Torso / waist / abdomen

- Torso length: **standard to slightly short for an adult woman**.
- Compact enough to support the petite silhouette, but not juvenile / compressed.
- Waist: **slim and clearly defined**.
- Ribcage → waist → pelvis transition is smooth and natural.
- Do not create an extreme corset-like pinch or independent exaggerated hourglass indentation.
- Anatomical waist position remains natural.
- Abdomen: **flat-leaning**, but not rigid board-flat.
- Preserve a small amount of natural softness, particularly through the lower abdomen into the pelvis.
- No default six-pack / fitness-model abdominal definition.

## 5. Pelvis / hips

- Pelvis width: natural adult-female baseline.
- Outer hip width: approximately similar to, or only slightly wider than, YURA's somewhat narrow shoulders.
- Hips: soft natural adult-female roundness.
- Lateral flare: restrained to natural.
- Waist → hip transition: gradual and smooth, not shelf-like.
- Side / back buttock projection: natural and moderate for a petite/slender adult woman.
- Neutral pelvic tilt; do not use swayback / exaggerated anterior tilt to increase apparent volume.
- Avoid both a childlike straight pelvis and an exaggerated glamour hourglass figure.

## 6. Legs

- Overall length: **standard to slightly long within the 7.25-head total**.
- Do not stretch toward 8+ head fashion-model proportions.
- Hip / buttock → upper-thigh line: smooth and natural.
- Upper thighs: **neither thick nor stick-thin**.
- Thighs: slender / delicate base with natural softness and gentle roundness.
- Avoid skeletal thighs, artificial thigh gaps and athletic muscle separation.
- Knees: small to modest / visually restrained, anatomically readable without knobby detail.
- Calves: slender with a gentle natural curve.
- Lower legs taper smoothly toward the ankles.
- Ankles: slim / delicate but anatomically plausible.
- Preserve the same thigh / calf proportions across front / side / back views.

## 7. Arms / hands — CORRECTED FROM EARLIER THINNESS

- Arms: **slender to slightly below average adult-female thickness**, not extremely thin.
- Earlier YURA generations tended to make the arms too thin; v1.0 deliberately restores a small amount of natural width / softness.
- Do not use arm thinness as the primary way to communicate `華奢`.
- Upper arms: slender but softly rounded; not bony / hollow / stick-like.
- Forearms: preserve natural thickness near the elbow and taper gradually toward the wrist.
- Wrists: slim / delicate but structurally plausible.
- Elbows: readable but visually restrained.
- Arm length: natural adult-female proportion.
- Hands: **small to standard** for a petite adult woman, not doll-small.
- Fingers: slightly long, slender and elegant, with soft taper and restrained joint detail.

## 8. Feet

- Foot size: **standard / natural for a 153 cm adult woman**.
- Do not make the feet extra small merely because YURA is petite.
- Foot width: standard.
- Instep / arch: standard and natural.
- Heel projection: ordinary / proportional.
- Toe shape: ordinary adult-female natural shape.
- Toes must not become pointed, doll-like, identical, needle-like or hyper-detailed.
- Do not exaggerate the big toe.
- Under the 2D anime rendering style, use enough structure to read as an adult foot while keeping joints / nails / skin detail restrained.

- SIDE must preserve the same ribcage, bust volume, abdomen, pelvis and limb proportions.

## 11. Neutral BODY-generation controls

When validating body proportions:

- one adult woman only
- full body, head top through toes
- standard perspective, minimal lens distortion
- natural upright posture
- **7.25-head ratio must remain invariant regardless of canvas dimensions**
- no glamour chest-forward pose
- no exaggerated contrapposto / pelvic tilt
- simple fitted reference clothing with modest exposure
- simple light neutral background
- soft diffuse light
- current 2D-first YURA rendering style
- no text, labels, panels, character sheet layout, callouts or typography unless explicitly requested

For a clean BODY reference, prefer:
- simple light inner top / camisole or plain fitted top
- simple shorts
- bare feet
- no unnecessary accessories

## 12. BODY negative constraints

Avoid:

- childlike large-head proportion
- 8+ head fashion-model elongation
- canvas-dependent head/body ratio drift
- anatomy being regenerated merely because image size / aspect ratio changed
- thicker thighs / calves caused by portrait-canvas reinterpretation
- broad / athletic shoulders
- muscular / visibly trained build
- oversized ribcage
- independently oversized bust
- implant-like spherical bust
- extreme corset waist
- exaggerated hourglass hips
- overly wide pelvis
- skeletal thighs
- oversized muscular calves
- stick-thin / skeletal arms
- doll-small hands / feet
- glamour posing that changes apparent proportions
- camera distortion that lengthens legs or enlarges head / bust
- anatomy changing between front / side / back
- realistic 3D / CGI anatomical treatment that conflicts with YURA's current 2D rendering authority
