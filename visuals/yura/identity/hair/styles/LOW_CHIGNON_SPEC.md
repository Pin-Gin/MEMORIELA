## 1. Trigger routing — mandatory

Apply this spec when the user requests:

- `ローシニヨン`
- `低いシニヨン`
- `low chignon`
- `full low chignon`

A side chignon, loose hybrid chignon, partially untucked chignon, or decorative variant requires an explicit separate request and must not silently replace this default.

---

## 2. Source-hair conservation

Low Chignon is constructed from YURA's protected Super-Long source hair.

Preserve:

- silver-white hair
- source length category = Super-Long
- total source mass = slightly above standard
- fine / soft strand character
- protected bangs
- protected long cheek-framing side bangs

A gathered style may conceal source length, but it must not delete it.

The stored length is represented through:
- wrapping
- folding
- overlapping
- tucking
- internal turns inside the chignon structure

---

## 3. Anatomical position — protected

The Low Chignon is centered on the **rear midline of the lower back of the head**.

Protected placement:

- lower rear head / lower occipital region
- around the lower edge of the skull
- may approach the upper edge of the nape
- remains fundamentally on the **back of the head**
- centered on the rear midline

Do not interpret `low` as placing the bun down onto the neck.

The bun must **not rest on, hang over, or substantially cover the neck itself**.

Practical interpretation:

**後頭部下部の正中、頭蓋骨下端付近〜うなじ上端に収める。首筋そのものには乗せない。**

Forbidden:
- beside one ear
- side of neck
- centered on the neck
- excessively high occipital bun
- lateral shift for camera readability

---

## 4. Full-storage topology — mandatory

For normal full Low Chignon:

- all non-face-framing side / back hair converges into one rear-center low chignon
- all Super-Long back hair is fully stored inside the chignon
- no full-length rear tail remains loose
- no long back strands hang below the chignon
- non-face-framing incidental wisps remain minimal
- protected bangs and intended face-framing strands remain outside

The arrangement must not read as:
**loose Super-Long hair + unrelated bun**

It must read as:
**the source Super-Long hair has been physically gathered and stored into one coherent low chignon.**

---

## 5. Bun definition / size — protected

The chignon itself is:

- **compact to small-medium**
- soft rather than rigid
- dense enough to credibly contain the stored Super-Long source hair
- not tiny enough to imply deleted hair mass
- not large enough to become an oversized fantasy sphere

Unlike Strong Braided Half-Up, this bun stores almost the entire non-face-framing source hair mass.
Therefore:

- it may be larger than the small half-up bun
- but it must still remain compact / controlled
- fine soft YURA hair may compress substantially
- source mass must remain credible through internal wrapping / folding rather than visible ballooning

Target impression:
**上品・柔らかい・落ち着いた、コンパクト〜中小のローシニヨン。**

---

## 6. Front-view projection — protected

In a true front-facing neutral view:

- the chignon is located behind the head
- the chignon should be **fully hidden or almost fully hidden**
- being invisible from the front is correct
- a very small natural edge may appear only if projection naturally allows it
- the front view does not need to prove the bun exists

The normal front-view expectation is:

**真正面では原則として見えない。**

Do not:
- move the bun sideways
- move it upward
- widen it
- expose part of it beside an ear
- leave long loose hair outside

merely to make the hairstyle readable.

Correct workflow:
**build the rear-center low chignon physically first, then accept whatever the front camera naturally sees.**

---

## 7. Side / three-quarter / back projection

### Three-quarter
- anatomical center remains rear-center
- apparent side offset may occur only from perspective
- do not relocate the bun toward the camera

### Side
- the low rear-head placement should become readable
- bun remains behind the skull
- bun does not drop onto the neck

### Back
- root convergence toward one lower rear-center chignon should be clearly readable
- stored mass should appear coherent
- no detached pasted-on bun
- no unexplained loose Super-Long tail

---

## 8. No visibility compensation — absolute

Physical construction first, camera projection second.

Do not compensate for hidden structure by changing:

- bun position
- bun size
- side-hair placement
- loose-hair amount
- head orientation
- camera
- BODY
- clothing

Do not expose hidden rear structure merely because the user must be able to "see the hairstyle."

If a correct front view hides the chignon, that is PASS.

---

## 9. Gravity / contact / collision

- roots converge softly toward the rear-center low gather
- the stored bun remains compact under gravity
- local compression against collar / headrest is allowed
- local contact must not relocate the entire chignon
- hair remains soft, fine and non-shellacked
- avoid clipping through neck, collar, chair, or head
