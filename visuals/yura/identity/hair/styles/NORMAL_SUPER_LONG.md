# YURA Normal Super-Long

Status: **PROTECTED DEFAULT HAIRSTYLE / HARD LENGTH LOCK**

## CRITICAL LENGTH LOCK
Do **not** interpret "Normal Super-Long" using generic anime conventions.

For YURA, the allowed length is exactly:

- **PRINCIPAL / MAIN HAIR MASS:** ends at the natural waistline to only slightly below it
- **ONLY A SMALL NUMBER OF FINEST LONGEST TIPS:** may approach just before the upper-buttock
- **DENSE / PRINCIPAL MASS MUST NOT REACH MID-BUTTOCK**
- **NO PRINCIPAL MASS AT LOWER BUTTOCK**
- **NO PRINCIPAL MASS AT THIGH**
- **NO THIGH-LENGTH HAIR**

If the main dense hair curtain reaches the buttocks or thighs:
`HAIR LENGTH FAIL`

The words "Super-Long" never override these boundaries.

## Length — forbidden interpretation

- 顔周りと胸前へ来る毛束は必要最小限
- 後ろ髪の大半を前へ移動しない

- 上臀部手前へ届くのはごく少数の細い毛先のみ

## Tips
- 細く柔らかく段階的にテーパー
- 重い一直線の切り揃えにしない
- スカスカの糸状にもならない

## HARD FAIL

- 主毛量が太ももへ到達
- 密な毛束がヒップ下まで伸びる
