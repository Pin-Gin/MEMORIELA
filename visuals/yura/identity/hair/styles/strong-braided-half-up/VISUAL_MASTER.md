## 3. Protected visual interpretation

Front view:

- side / upper braids may be visible
- their flow converges backward
- actual gather / tie point remains behind the head and may be hidden
- hidden structure must not be exposed merely for readability
- no paired side knots / twin-bun interpretation

### Rear-center bun interpretation

When the gather becomes naturally visible from side / three-quarter / back views:

- it resolves as a **small compact bun at the center rear of the head**
- it remains visually subordinate to the braid structure
- it must not become oversized
- it must not shift laterally or upward merely for readability
- the braids connect continuously into this rear-center bun
- the bun size must remain consistent with the fact that part of the gathered hair mass is already consumed by the braid structure

Length / mass:

- principal ends remain around natural waistline to slightly below
- only longest fine ends may approach the upper-buttock boundary
- principal mass must not extend to mid-buttock / thighs
- total source mass remains only slightly above standard
- lower loose section must not be inflated

Luxury:

- generated through braid structure
- restrained ornament may be added only when the derivative request calls for it
- never create luxury by increasing length / width / total mass
