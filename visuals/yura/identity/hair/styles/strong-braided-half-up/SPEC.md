## 2. Absolute source-length lock

Strong Braided Half-Upは、YURAのprotected Super-Long source hairを加工したarrangementである。

元のスーパーロング長を厳守する。

- 主な毛先: **自然なウエストライン付近〜わずかに下**
- 最長の細い毛先のみ: **上臀部手前まで許容**
- 主要毛量を**臀部中央以下へ延長しない**
- 主要毛量を**太ももまで延長しない**
- dominant lengthを胸下付近まで短縮しない
- arrangementを理由にsource lengthを増減しない

豪華さを出す目的で髪を長くしてはならない。

---

## 3. Total mass lock

HAIR v1.3のsource total massをそのまま維持する。

- 総毛量: **標準よりやや多めまで**
- 一本一本: 細く柔らかい
- fine hairをsparse hairとして解釈しない
- half-up化しても総毛量を増やさない
- 下ろしている部分を増量しない
- 編み込みを追加するために別の毛束を外部から足したような構造にしない
- 頭頂 / 側頭部の過度な膨張で豪華さを表現しない
- 横方向へ扇状に広げて豪華さを表現しない

**豪華さを長さ・横幅・毛量で表現しない。**

---

## 4. Half-up topology

Strong Braided Half-Upはhalf-up familyとして構築する。

- gathered section = 上部のみ
- おおむね耳上ライン / 上側頭部から後頭部上部の髪を使用
- lower section = 十分な量を保った元のSuper-Long loose hair
- lower sectionのsource massを薄くしない
- lower sectionを増量もしない
- upper gathered hairは後頭部中央へ自然に収束する
- anatomical gather pointを正面から見せるために左右へ移動しない

Bangs / face framing:

- protected thin long bangsを維持
- protected long cheek-framing side bangsを維持
- 編み込み構造のために前髪 / 顔まわりを消失させない

## 4A. Rear-center small bun / knot — protected

Strong Braided Half-Up の結束部は、**後頭部中央でまとめた小さめのお団子状**を正とする。

### Position

- 結束位置は **後頭部の中心**
- 左右へ寄せない
- 上方へ持ち上げすぎない
- 襟足まで下げすぎない
- head rear-center axis 上で自然に収まる

### Form

- 結束部は **小さめのお団子状**
- 存在は明確だが、主張しすぎない
- giant bun / oversized knot にしない
- twin-bun / paired-knot にしない
- decorative mass inflation で大きく見せない

### Mass logic

YURAの source hair は「標準よりやや多め」だが、Strong Braided Half-Up では:

1. 上部の髪をhalf-upとして取り分ける
2. その一部を編み込み構造として使用する
3. 下ろした部分のsource massは増量しない

したがって、結束部のお団子は **compact / small** を基準とする。

編み込みに使われた毛量を無視して、通常ハーフアップより大きなbunを作ってはならない。

### Front-view behavior

正面では、このrear-center bun / knotは頭部に隠れるため、**基本的に見えなくてよい**。

正面から読み取る主情報は:

- 左右上部の編み込み
- 編み込みが後方へ流れること
- rear-centerへ収束していく方向性

であり、結束部そのものを露出させる必要はない。

### Side / three-quarter / back behavior

側面・斜め・背面など、後頭部が自然に見える角度では:

- **後頭部中央の小さめなお団子**として見える
- 左右へ逃がさない
- 上へ持ち上げない
- 二つの結び目に分裂させない
- bunを見せるためにカメラ・毛流れ・頭部位置を補正しない

### Braid connection

左右の編み込みは、**後頭部中央の小さめのお団子結束へ自然に接続・収束する**。

編み込み終点と結束部は同一のhair topologyとして連続していなければならない。

### Prohibited

- bun / knot を見せるための lateral shift
- bun / knot を見せるための upward shift
- 正面から見せるための artificial protrusion
- left/right duplicated knots
- source mass と不釣り合いな oversized bun
- luxuryをbun size / extra massで表現すること

**実務短縮解釈:  
結束部は後頭部中央の小さめなお団子。正面では見えなくてよく、見える角度のみ自然に見える。見せるための位置補正・巨大化・左右分裂は禁止。**

## 5. Strong braid construction

`編み込み強め` means:

- upper side sections contain **clearly readable braid structure**
- braids / twists flow continuously from the upper sides toward the rear-center gather
- braid pattern is visually more explicit than a simple half-up
- braid remains anatomically integrated into the actual hair flow
- do not paste a detached braid onto otherwise unchanged loose hair
- do not multiply braid count until the hairstyle becomes a different character design
- do not sacrifice YURA's protected bangs, face framing, source length or source mass

The arrangement should read as:
**YURA's existing Super-Long hair, partially gathered into a clearly structured braided half-up.**

---

## 6. Luxury / ornament rule

豪華さは主に以下で出す。

1. **編み込み構造の明確さ / complexity**
2. **必要に応じた上品なhair ornament**

Allowed ornament direction:

- small-to-moderate refined hair ornaments
- restrained ribbon
- delicate metallic ornament
- small floral / filigree accent
- outfitに合わせた色・素材調整

Rules:

- ornament must not redefine YURA identity
- ornament must not force a larger hairstyle silhouette
- ornament must not increase source hair length / source hair mass
- ornament is subordinate to physical braid construction
- explicit `装飾なし` / `hair ornamentなし` request overrides ornament use
- if decoration is unspecified, keep it restrained rather than extravagant

---

## 7. Camera / visibility / no-compensation rule

Physical construction first, camera projection second.

### Front-view protected interpretation

In a true front-facing neutral view:

- the actual gather / tie point is at the **rear-center of the head** and is normally hidden or nearly hidden by the skull / rear hair
- the viewer may see the **side braids / upper gathered flow traveling backward toward the hidden rear-center**
- the front view does **not** require visible paired side knots, side buns, or duplicated tie points
- if the image appears to show two separate visible knots / gather points at the left and right sides, treat that as a topology / visibility-compensation failure unless explicitly requested as a twin arrangement
- do not invent lateral decorative hair masses merely to prove that the hairstyle is half-up
- correct front-view readability comes from visible backward convergence of the braided sections, not from exposing the hidden rear tie

General:

- rear-center gather / braid may be partly or completely hidden from the front
- unseen rear structure does not need to be artificially exposed
- do not shift the arrangement sideways merely to show the braid
- do not bring rear hair forward merely to prove the length
- do not split the loose hair widely merely to show BODY / waist / clothing
- do not reduce length / mass to reveal outfit construction
- do not add hair on the visible side to compensate for hidden hair
- occluded portions remain occluded when physically appropriate

**ポージング・カメラ・衣装による見えない部分の補正、見せるための補正をしない。**

## 8. Gravity / silhouette

- upper hair settles naturally under the weight of the source length
- gathered section has soft natural tension, not shellacked rigidity
- loose lower section falls primarily downward under gravity
- lateral spread remains restrained
- tips taper gradually / delicately
- no giant fan-shaped silhouette
- no mid-buttock / thigh-length principal mass

## 12. QA — arrangement-specific Gate 4 checks

PASS requires:

- FACE / expression remains neutral-to-soft and consistent with current YURA baseline
- BODY presentation remains within current protected baseline without compensation
- validation clothing passes `docs/assistant-context/creation/yura/qa/VALIDATION_CLOTHING_SPEC.md` and remains plain pale opaque sleeveless top + plain pale opaque simple shorts
- source silver-white Super-Long identity preserved
- principal ends remain around waistline to slightly below
- only longest fine tips may approach upper-buttock boundary
- no principal mass at mid-buttock / thighs
- total mass remains only slightly above standard
- lower loose section is neither artificially increased nor excessively thinned
- upper section clearly forms a half-up
- braid structure is clearly readable
- gathered flow converges physically toward rear-center
- when visible, the rear-center gather resolves as a **small compact bun**, not a large bun or paired side knots
- face-framing / bangs remain protected
- luxury comes from structure / restrained ornament rather than mass inflation
- no camera-driven relocation / visibility compensation

FAIL examples:

- expression becomes visibly harsher / sterner than neutral YURA during a hairstyle-only test
- clothing materially changes category or becomes fashion-oriented rather than neutral validation clothing
- clothing fit or styling materially changes BODY comparability as a side effect of hairstyle generation
- front view shows two apparent side knots / duplicated gather points even though the arrangement should converge to one hidden rear-center gather
- visible rear gather becomes an oversized bun inconsistent with the available half-up / braided source mass
- bun is shifted sideways or upward merely to make it visible
- front view exposes a rear tie by shifting / duplicating it laterally
- hair becomes thigh length
- hair becomes substantially wider / fuller than current source mass
- braid appears detached / pasted on
- lower loose section doubles in volume
- rear gather moved beside the ear only for front visibility
- back hair pulled forward solely to expose body / garment
- hairstyle loses YURA's protected bangs / face-framing
